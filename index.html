Privacy Policy – Zakath

Last updated: May 2025

1. Data We Collect

Zakath does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter (gold amounts, cash savings, calculation history) is stored exclusively on your device using local storage and is never sent to any server.

2. Internet Access

The app connects to the internet solely to fetch the current gold and silver market prices for display purposes. No user data is included in these requests.

3. Location Permission

The app may request optional location access to display your city's local gold rate. This permission is entirely optional — the app works fully without it. Location data is used only in-session to determine your city and is never stored or transmitted.

4. Third-Party Services

Gold and silver prices are fetched from publicly available market data sources. No account, login, or personal information is required or sent to these sources.

5. Children

This app does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.

6. Changes

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.
